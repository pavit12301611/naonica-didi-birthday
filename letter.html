A Letter for my Didi

My Dearest Naonica Didi,
18th August 2026 • With all my love
♡

Happy Birthday, Didi! Today the world feels a little softer, a little brighter — because it’s the day you were born. If I could wrap all my love into words, I’d write you a library, but let me try to tell you in this one letter what you mean to me.

You’ve been my first teacher, my fiercest protector, my softest safe place. I still remember how you’d fix my hair before school, scold me when I was being lazy, and then secretly slip me that extra chocolate when no one was watching. You’ve celebrated my tiny wins like they were big festivals and held my hand through the days I didn’t even know how to ask for help.

Didi, your strength amazes me. The way you handle everything with grace — your smile even when you’re tired, your calm even when things are messy, your love even when we’re being difficult — it’s nothing short of magic. You make “being there” look effortless, but I know how much heart you pour into our family every single day.

I hope today you feel even a fraction of the love you give us. You deserve flowers without a reason, surprises for no occasion, and all the mithai, laughter, and music your heart wants. You deserve rest, you deserve dreams that come true, you deserve people who see and cherish how rare you are.

On your birthday, my only prayer is this: May your year be gentle on your heart, kind to your dreams, and full of reasons to smile that big, beautiful smile of yours. May health, happiness and success follow you wherever you go — and may you never forget how deeply, endlessly loved you are.

Thank you for being my didi. Thank you for being you. No gift in this little website can ever match what you’ve given me — but I hope when you scroll through it, you feel hugged. Because that’s what this is — a big, warm, tight hug from me to you.

I love you, Didi. More than words, more than pages, more than birthdays can count.

Forever your —
Your little one who adores you
— with love, cuddles, and endless respect ♡
